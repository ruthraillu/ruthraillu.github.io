Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026

auc-drive-shared is a private, self-hosted personal automation tool operated exclusively by its owner to access and store files in the owner's own Google Drive account.

Data Usage

  • This application accesses Google Drive solely as authorized by the user through Google OAuth 2.0 to upload, organize, and read files within the user's Drive.
  • No user data, OAuth tokens, or Drive contents are collected, sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties.
  • Credentials and refresh tokens are stored locally on the owner's private devices/servers.
